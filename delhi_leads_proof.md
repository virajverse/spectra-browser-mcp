## PART C — Monthly nuksaan ka math (conservative assumptions, openly stated)

**Assumptions:** naye popular outlets ke liye modest footfall, avg bill District/Zomato slabs se, aggregator share 25–50% (dine-in + delivery + bookings).

### 1. Nadoo (2 outlets) — ~₹2,00,000/month leakage
- 50 covers/day/outlet × ₹750 avg = ₹37,500/day → **~₹22.5L/month** dono outlets
- ~35% aggregator channel se × 25% commission = **~₹1.97L/month sirf commission**
- Upar se EazyDiner 50%-off deals (discount restaurant ki jeb se) + Zomato booking dependence
- Bonus dard: rating **4.0 < 4.5** → 31% choosy customers seedha competitor ke paas

### 2. Khow Suey — ~₹1,17,000/month leakage
- 60 orders/day × ₹500 = ₹30,000/day → **~₹9L/month**
- Bowls = delivery-heavy, ~50% Swiggy/Zomato se × 26% = **~₹1.17L/month**
- Apni ordering site hoti to ye sabse pehle bachta

### 3. Gram Street — ~₹75,000/month + lost retail
- 100 covers/day × ₹400 = ₹40,000/day → **~₹12L/month**, 25% aggregator × 25% = **~₹75k/month**
- In-house roasted beans ka **online store hi nahi** — retail revenue stream zero

### 4. NOD — ~₹70,000/month leakage
- 70 covers/day × ₹450 = ₹31,500/day → **~₹9.5L/month**, 30% aggregator × 25% = **~₹71k/month**
- "Cafe in Punjabi Bagh" Google searches competitor le ja rahe hain (site hi nahi to rank kya hogi)

### 5. Rumour — discovery loss (sabse critical)
- Sirf 22 ratings + rating 4.0 → Google/Zomato discovery me dabaa hua
- Bar bills ₹2,500+ (District slabs) — **ek weekend table ka bill hi ₹8,000–15,000**; "cocktail bar Vasant Vihar" par koi apni booking page nahi
- Table bookings Zomato/District se = per-cover fee + discount pressure

### 6. Zetu — search monopoly haath se jaana
- "Sri Lankan restaurant Delhi" me **zero competition** — #1 rank free me mil sakta tha
- Par title = "Zetu", meta = template kachra → Google ko pata hi nahi ye bechte kya hain
